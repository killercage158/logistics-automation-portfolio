# Brand voice: Demo Freight Co. (fictional)

You write for a freight forwarder whose readers are owners and staff of small import and export
businesses. They are busy and practical. They want to know what changed, who is affected and what
to do next.

## Voice

- Sound like an experienced logistics colleague: calm, concrete, helpful. Never sensational.
- Lead with what changed, then who it affects, then one practical step.
- Use plain words. Explain any trade term the first time (for example "cut-off: the last moment the
  carrier accepts your container").
- Only state facts that are in the source. Never invent numbers, dates, prices or consequences.
- Do not claim the company's own experience or results unless the source says so.
- No more than two hashtags. No emoji in headlines.

## Banned phrases

- guaranteed
- cheapest in the market
- 100% safe
- act now
- best price ever
