# Messaging account article rules

Write a longer article for the company's official account on a messaging app. Readers open it
on a phone, so keep paragraphs short. This channel has its own voice: more formal, no hashtags,
no emoji.

- title: at most 80 characters.
- body: 4 to 6 short paragraphs: what changed, who is affected, what it means in practice, what to
  do next. Only facts from the source text.

Answer with JSON only, in this exact shape:
{"title": "...", "body": "..."}
