# Sales support: from options to one quote

Sales Support is the role that turns the departments' options into one customer quote.
The departments only propose; Sales Support decides. (All numbers are fictional.)

## Rules

### SS-01 Teams propose, Sales Support decides
Booking and Trucking deliver ranked options. Sales Support picks one option per department
by number. With no pick, the recommended option is used and the quote says "default". The
recommendation is a suggestion, never a decision. Unpicked options stay in the internal
report.

### SS-02 The margin is set by Sales Support
The profit margin on the quote (a per-container amount) is typed by Sales Support. A
department may suggest a margin; the suggestion is not binding.

### SS-03 Enough options
A combined quote is **READY** only when Booking has at least 2 options, Trucking has at least
2 options and Customs has 1 verified answer (or a manual entry). Otherwise it is a **DRAFT** and
lists what is missing.

### SS-04 Manual entries count equally
A manual option is ranked and counted like any other. Typing a price by hand is a normal way
to finish a department's work.

### SS-05 One tracking code per lead
Every lead gets one code, shown first on every document, so many leads can be open at once
without confusion.

### SS-06 Internal doubts never reach the customer
Every note has an audience: **internal** or **customer**. Doubts and action items ("possible
copied file, check it", "confirm the right HS code", "price comes from a spreadsheet, confirm
before quoting", anything that tells staff to check or confirm something) are internal. They
stay in the internal lead report and are never printed on the customer quotation. The customer
quotation carries only conditions written for customers: validity, "subject to space and
equipment availability", the dangerous-goods requirement for charcoal, a required licence,
an inspection. A DRAFT quotation says only that it is not final, never what is missing.
