# Free time (demurrage and detention days)

How many days a container may stay at the origin port (POL) and the destination port
(POD) before fees start. This guide is the only source for the numbers.
(All numbers in this demo are fictional.)

## Citation (required)

The guide's name and the table row (or page and sentence) that holds the number of days.

## Rules

### FT-01 Free time comes only from the carrier's guide
Use the carrier's published free-time guide, for every carrier, for both ends. Do not use
a figure from a live carrier quote, a price file or memory, even if it looks newer. Staff
check free time against the guide; a number from anywhere else would look wrong to them.

### FT-02 Exceptions beat defaults
A guide may list defaults for a carrier and exceptions for one port or one cargo type.
The most specific row wins: port + cargo, then port, then cargo, then the carrier
default. The option says when an exception was used.

### FT-03 A missing carrier is flagged
If the guide has no entry for the carrier (or for one end), do not guess and do not remove
the option. Show "free time not found in the guides, ask the carrier".
